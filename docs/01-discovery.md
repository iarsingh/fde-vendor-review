# Discovery — Northline Procurement

The user is the vendor-setup analyst. A packet needs a W-9, an insurance date that is still in the future, and a second person whenever the bank details changed. Sanctions hits are not a judgment call for this desk.

Constraint from legal: the software must not contain an approve action. Constraint from finance: the person who typed the bank change cannot be the only person who releases it.

Out of scope: paying an invoice, onboarding in the ERP, and overriding a sanctions flag inside the tool.
